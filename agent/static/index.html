One advisory in. A proven fix for every service out.

Lighthouse finds the services a new vulnerability really affects, fixes them, and proves the fix worked. It asks a person when policy says so.

  • Runs on your own servers
  • Every approval is recorded by name
  • Rolls back on any failed step

Sign in

Paste your access token. Your approvals are recorded under the name it belongs to.

Where do I find my token?
  • Ask whoever runs Lighthouse; they set one per person in PP_API_TOKENS.
  • Running it yourself? The agent prints a token when it starts.
  • A link ending in #token=… signs you in once, and the browser remembers it.
